=====================================================================
          SKY VOICE CRAFT - CLIENT QUICK START GUIDE
=====================================================================

1. HOW TO RUN:
   - For Laptop / PC Studio View: Double-click 'Laptop_Version.bat'
   - For Smartphone App View:     Double-click 'Phone_Version.bat'

2. FIRST TIME SETUP:
   - Open the app.
   - Click on the top right 'API Vault' or the key icon.
   - Paste your free Google AI Studio API Key (Get from: https://aistudio.google.com/app/apikey).
   - Click 'Save & Verify API Profile'.

3. READY TO GENERATE:
   - Paste your story or narration script.
   - Pick your favorite voice persona (25+ HD voices).
   - Click 'GENERATE HD AI VOICE'.
   - Play audio and click 'Download HD MP3'.
